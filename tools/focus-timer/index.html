ADHD Focus Timer — Built for Brains That Won't Cooperate

Standard Pomodoro timers assume you can sit down and focus for 25 minutes. ADHD brains know that's rarely how it works. This free ADHD timer is designed around how neurodivergent focus actually happens — short bursts, visual feedback, and satisfying rewards.

How to Use This Timer for ADHD Focus

1. Pick the shortest duration you can commit to. 5 minutes? That counts. Starting is the hardest part — the timer handles the rest.

2. Focus on one thing. Close extra tabs. Put your phone face-down. One task, one timer.

3. When the timer ends, decide: Take a break, or hit "+5 more minutes" if you're in the zone.

4. Track your streak. Consistency beats intensity. A 5-minute daily streak beats one 3-hour session per month.

The Modified ADHD Pomodoro Technique

The traditional Pomodoro Technique (25 min work / 5 min break) works great for neurotypical brains. For ADHD, the modification is simple: start shorter. Use 10–15 minute focus sessions instead of 25. The key insight is that ADHD task initiation is the bottleneck — once you're focused, you can always extend. That's why this timer defaults to 15 minutes and gives you the "+5 more" button.
